# Closure as a translational truth condition

## Correction of the working language

Closure is not the preservation of a closed truth, a quality score, a quantity,
or an entropy value. It is the truth condition for a relation to continue
through a reciprocal translation.

Let a local formation be presented by `X`, a further formation by `Y`, a
chosen translation by `T : X -> Y`, and a return by `rho : Y -> X`. The
relevant assertion is not that an isolated sentence or state keeps a permanent
truth label. It is that an admitted relation has a truthful further
realization:

```text
Admit(X, T, rho, Y)
  only if the chosen translation, its return, and their relation are auditable
  and the result supplies a next admissible relation.
```

The local/global, finite/infinite, and operational/contextual readings are
therefore equal only **in the truth of their reciprocal relation**. They are
not thereby identical objects, equally measurable quantities, or versions of
one fixed language.

## Translation, not closed-truth preservation

The intended form is:

```text
formation X
  -- chosen T --> formation Y
  -- independent rho --> returned relation R(X, rho(T(X)))
  -- admitted continuation --> formation X'
```

`R` can hold while `rho(T(X)) != X` as an ordered presentation. A residue may
remain; it records the orientation, route, or constraint carried into `X'`.
Thus a successful closure can be `CLOSED_TO_NEW_OPENING`, not a frozen truth.

Conversely, a zero residue at one declared level is not finality. It may only
express a parent relation, while child orientations or route histories remain
distinct. No inference from a finite zero to an absolute `0 = infinity` is
licensed by the current formal work.

## Admission and natural choice

An admission gate is not a closed entropy barrier and must not certify itself.
It is a truth-condition interface:

```text
chosen interaction
  -> independent return / consequence
  -> relation of the admission to its declared constraints
  -> admitted continuation | obstruction | OPEN
```

Naturality is not imposed before choice. A choice is first made and exposed;
its compatibility, reflection, recovery, and independently generated
consequences are then tested. An admission is truthful only relative to that
auditable relation. A self-issued receipt, a missing return, or a gate verdict
reused as its own external outcome is not a successful admission.

This is why the native Level-1 and Level-2 controls separate structural gate
behaviour, independent calibration, and external grounding. They do **not**
yet establish a universal truth oracle.

## Transformation cost

The requested universal cost is not yet a number. Its intended invariant role
is the proof-relevant burden of carrying identity across a translation without
privileging a language or definition basis:

```text
Cost(T, rho) = retained obligations, failed obligations, and non-erasable
               residues needed to make the next relation admissible.
```

Any later scalar, order, or resource model must be shown invariant under
admitted re-expression and compositional under sequential translations. Until
then, the repository uses explicit witnesses, counterexamples, abstentions,
and residues—not entropy or a universal numerical cost.

## Formal status and next work

This is a foundational interpretation and interface specification. Existing
bounded controls show selected parts of it: relational return without literal
identity, residue-sensitive continuation, independent gate calibration, and
external-outcome boundaries. They do not prove language-prior universal truth.

The remaining formal opening is a proof-relevant, pre-institutional relation
interface in which a transformation-cost object can be defined and tested for:

1. invariance under re-expression;
2. composition across successive translations;
3. independent return and consequence witnesses;
4. explicit obstruction and OPEN cases; and
5. level-relative zero/inverse-limit claims without collapsing finite history.

This refines rather than replaces [Open translational foundation](OPEN_TRANSLATIONAL_FOUNDATION.md)
and [Pre-institutional translation openings](PREINSTITUTIONAL_TRANSLATION_OPENINGS.md).
