# Closure as a translational truth condition

## Canonical correction

Closure is about **truth at the relational level**, not equality of quality,
quantity, syntax, coordinate value, or entropy. Local/global and `0`/`∞` may be
distinct presentations while being equal **in the truth of the closure
relation** that carries them into one another.

Accordingly, the thesis is not ordinary arithmetic `0 = ∞`, nor the claim that
a local quantity equals a global quantity. It is:

```text
presentation-local distinction
        +
truth-level relational equality
        +
translation into a further relation.
```

In closure notation, one may write schematically

```text
0 != ∞                     as local presentations
[0]_C = [∞]_C              at the truth/closure relation
```

without collapsing their relative orientation, magnitude, role, or language.
The equality belongs to the relational truth under closure, not to ordinary
numerical identity.

## Translation, not preservation of a finished truth

Closure is not the preservation of a closed truth-object. A truthful relation
is translated into a **further relation**.

Let a local formation be presented by `X`, a further formation by `Y`, a
chosen translation by `T : X -> Y`, and a return by `rho : Y -> X`. The
intended structure is:

```text
formation X
  -- chosen T --> formation Y
  -- independent rho --> returned relation R(X, rho(T(X)))
  -- admitted continuation --> further relation / formation X'
```

The important claim is therefore not

```text
truth(X) is copied unchanged into Y,
```

but rather

```text
the truth of the relation remains admissible through translation into X'.
```

`R` can hold while `rho(T(X)) != X` as an ordered presentation. A residue may
remain; it records orientation, route, constraint, or newly disclosed
relational structure. Successful closure can therefore be
`CLOSED_TO_NEW_OPENING` rather than a frozen fixed point.

This is also why a recovery-only test is too weak. A frozen identity round and
a genuine translating round can both recover locally. Translation additionally
requires actual movement relative to the admitted relation and explicit
lineage into the next level.

## Language independence

The truth relation is not defined by selecting one origin language and then
forcing every other presentation into it. Languages, definitions, local
geometries, and axiom systems are relative presentations of a more primitive
translational relation.

A cross-frame statement such as

```text
W_m(T_lm u) = phi_lm(W_l u)
```

is therefore read as a formal witness that two local presentations participate
in one recoverable relational identity. The formal square is not itself a
proof that translation is metaphysically prior to language; that priority is
the foundational interpretation being tested.

## Admission is a truth condition on natural choice

The potential gate should not be interpreted primarily as a closed entropy
barrier or as a device whose job is to minimize uncertainty. It is a
**truth-condition interface on a choice**.

A choice is made first. Its admission is then tested by whether the chosen
interaction can enter a natural further relation under the declared
constraints, return, and independently generated consequences:

```text
choice
  -> proposed translation
  -> independent return / consequence
  -> relation of the choice to the current and further frame
  -> admitted continuation | obstruction | OPEN
```

Thus true admission must itself be related to its natural choice. Naturality
is not a pre-choice oracle and not a hidden global selector; it is the
post-choice condition that the chosen translation commutes, recovers, reflects,
and remains externally auditable at the required level.

A self-issued receipt, a missing return, a gate verdict reused as its own
calibration signal, or an outcome derived from the gate verdict is not a
truthful admission. The native Level-1 and Level-2 controls enforce these
anti-circular boundaries without claiming a universal truth oracle.

## Universal transformation cost

The universal transformation cost sought by the programme is **not** a cost of
one syntax, language, coordinate chart, definition basis, or entropy encoding.
It is the transformation burden of the relational identity itself.

The desired object therefore has to factor through translational identity:

```text
presentation-level transformation
        -> closure/translational identity class
        -> transformation cost.
```

If two presentation-level maps are admitted re-expressions of the same
relational transformation, then the eventual cost must agree:

```text
T ~_C T'  ->  Cost(T) = Cost(T').
```

At present the repository does **not** claim a universal scalar formula. It
records the proof-relevant constituents from which such a cost would have to
be derived: retained obligations, failed obligations, non-erasable residues,
return requirements, external-audit burdens, abstentions, and explicit
obstructions.

Any later numerical or ordered cost must satisfy at least:

1. **language/definition invariance** — re-expression does not change cost;
2. **identity dependence** — cost belongs to the relational transformation,
   not its local encoding;
3. **composition** — sequential translations combine coherently;
4. **choice sensitivity** — different natural choices may incur different
   transformation burdens without making one language absolute;
5. **external auditability** — a zero cost cannot be manufactured by
   self-certification or leaked outcomes.

This is distinct from the bounded `sorting_cost` controls. Those controls test
one operational cost model for evaluating attempts; they are not the universal
transformation-cost claim.

## Relation to 0 / infinity and local / global

The programme's metaphysical reading is:

```text
local != global            as relative presentations
0 != ∞                     as relative presentations
local = global in closure  at truth-level relational identity
0 = ∞ in closure           at truth-level relational identity
```

The phrase “in closure” is essential. It names the equality level at which the
relation is true. It does not assert equality of ordinary quantities or erase
the relative poles. Their difference remains available as orientation,
residue, or further relational structure.

## Formal and experimental status

This document states the canonical metaphysical interpretation and the target
interfaces for future formalization. Existing Lean modules and bounded native
controls establish conditional and finite pieces: relational return without
literal identity, recovery-versus-translation separation, residue-sensitive
development, independent gate calibration, held-out external-consequence
audits, and successor-frame generation from completed relations.

They do **not** yet prove:

- a language-prior universal truth theorem;
- a universal `0 = ∞` theorem in ordinary mathematics;
- a universal scalar transformation cost;
- that every choice admits a natural continuation;
- or that a bounded external fixture grounds truth in the world.

The executable research target is instead a continuing closure cycle:

```text
truth-relative formation F_t
  -> natural choice / interaction
  -> translation
  -> independent return
  -> completion
  -> derived further relation F_(t+1)
  -> externally audited consequence
  -> next natural choice.
```
