## The instruction

> unify closure

## What was done

Until now every module built its own closure by hand — the hair of the ball, the handedness of
life, the phase read through the self limit — each with its own quotient and its own universal
property, and nothing said they were the same construction. This module defines the closure
**once**, for an arbitrary return `step : X → X`, and shows the earlier ones are instances of it.

* `Closure step` is the quotient of `X` by the equivalence generated by `b = step a`, with closure
  map `cl step`. A reading `f : X → Y` is *closed* when `f ∘ step = f`.
* **One universal property**: every closed reading factors through `cl step` in exactly one way
  (`lift`, `lift_cl`, `lift_unique`).
* **One uniqueness theorem**: `IsClosure step p` says `p : X → C` is closed and has that factoring
  property; `closure_unique` proves that any two objects with it are related by *exactly one*
  isomorphism commuting with the closure maps. This is what licenses the identification of all the
  separately built closures.
* **Functoriality**: `map`, `map_cl`, `map_id`, `map_comp`.

## The earlier closures as instances

* `hair_isClosure`, `hair_eq_closure` — NRRF800's hair *is* the closure of the ball's translation.
* `hand_isClosure`, `closure_ballReturn_hand` — the closure of life by the actual (ball) return is
  exactly its handedness.
* `phase_isClosure`, `closure_selfLimit_phase` — the closure of life by the self limit is exactly
  its ball phase.
* `unified_cardinalities` — the three closures count `1`, `2`, `4`.

## Closure unified with itself

* `Closure₂ f g` closes by two returns at once, with its own universal property (`lift₂`,
  `lift₂_cl₂`, `lift₂_unique`).
* `unify_closure` — for commuting returns, closing by both at once is the closure of the closure:
  `Closure₂ f g ≃ Closure (push f g)`, by a unique isomorphism over `X`.
* `unify_closure_symm` — the order of the two returns does not matter.

## Unified life

* `closure₂_life_subsingleton`, `unified_life_card_one` — closing life by *both* of its returns
  leaves exactly one point, the hair again.
* `unified_life_tower` — the same one point reached as a tower (actual return, then the pushed
  potential return).
* `relations_unified` — every relation reading of every configuration lands in that single point.
* `closure_hand_inv_subsingleton` — the closure of the two hands by the inversion is one point.

`nrrf802_answer` collects the clauses into a single theorem.
