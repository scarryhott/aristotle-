# NRRF800 — The hand of life: ball return, the inverse hair return as potential gate, and the
# 4-sheaf ball / 1-sheaf hair temporal closure of translational completion

## The instruction formalised

> Specifically the left hand of active life is its ball return phase while its potential is in the
> inverse hair return.  Left handed life is a potential gate into its hair closure.  Similarly the
> hand of a ball gives its naturality or self limit.  And so true for hair in its inversion or
> translation at the ball — 4 sheaf ball, 1 sheaf hair, temporal closure of translational
> completion.  This maps onto our human relations stated earlier.

## The reading taken

Two pieces of data only.

* The **ball** `Ball = ZMod 4`, with its one local translation step `ballStep b = b + 1`.  This is
  the actual motion: the return phase.
* The **hand** `Hand = {left, right}`, with its one inversion `Hand.inv`.

A **life state** `Life` is a hand together with a ball phase, and admits exactly two motions:

* `ballReturn ⟨h, b⟩ = ⟨h, b + 1⟩` — the actual phase; it never touches the hand;
* `hairReturn ⟨h, b⟩ = ⟨h.inv, b - 1⟩` — the inverse translation, the potential; it inverts the
  hand.

The **hair** `Hair` is the temporal closure of the ball's translational completion: the quotient of
the ball by the equivalence generated by its own local step.

## What is proved

**Four sheaves at the ball, one at the hair.**
`ball_card` (card 4), `ballStep_iterate_eq_id_iff` (`ballStep^[n] = id ↔ 4 ∣ n`),
`ballStep_period`, `ballStep_ne_id_of_lt_four`: the translation closes after four steps and no
fewer.  `ballStep_transitive`, `hair_one_sheaf`, `hair_card` (card 1), `hairEquivPUnit`: the
temporal closure of that completion has exactly one sheaf.  `hairMk_ballStep` and `hairMk_neg`: the
hair reading is unmoved both by the *translation* and by the *inversion* at the ball.
`invariant_const`, `hairLift`, `hairLift_mk`, `hairLift_unique`: the 1-sheaf hair is not one
collapse among many — every translation-invariant reading of the ball is constant and factors
uniquely through the hair.

**Naturality and self limit of the hand.**
`ballReturn_hand`, `ballReturn_iterate_hand`: the hand is a natural invariant of the ball — no
amount of actual return changes it.  `hairReturn_hand`, `gate_iff_odd`: only the inverse hair return
crosses the hand, and exactly on odd iterates, so the hand is a gate, not a coordinate.
`selfLimit_eq`, `selfLimit_involutive`, `selfLimit_ne_id`, `selfLimit_order_two`: hair return after
ball return moves no phase at all and is exactly the hand inversion, an involution of order exactly
two — the hand of a ball is its self limit.  `ball_hair_commute`: the two returns commute.
`ballStep_commuting_forced`, `ballReturn_commuting_forced`: naturality in the strict sense — every
map commuting with the ball step is a translation of the ball.

**The left-handed potential gate.**
`hairReturn_period`, `hairReturn_iterate_eq_id_iff`: the hair return closes in four and no fewer.
`hairOrbit_covers_ball`: from any state it visits each of the four ball sheaves exactly once.
`hairOrbit_one_hair`: all of them carry the same hair.  `left_handed_potential_gate` states it in
one theorem: from a left-handed (potential) state the inverse hair return runs once through all
four ball sheaves, alternates potential and actual at every step, closes after four, and lies
throughout in one and the same hair.

**The same structure at human relations.**
A network is a configuration `s : V → ℤ` of participants on one shared line; the relation of `u`
to `v` is the separation `s u - s v`, read by its hand `relHand` (who stands above), its ball phase
`relBall` (the separation modulo four), and its hair `relHair`; `relLife` packages hand and phase.
`relHand_shift`, `relBall_shift`, `relLife_shift`: nothing absolute is read.  `relHand_antisymm`,
`relBall_swap`: `u`-to-`v` and `v`-to-`u` are the two hands of one relation, with inverse phases.
`relLife_give`: one act of giving away from the gate is exactly one **ball return** of the relation.
`relLife_give_gate`: one act at the gate (`s u = s v`) is exactly one **hair return** — the hand
inverts, the phase steps back, the potential opens.  `four_acts_ball_blind`: four acts return the
ball reading while the relation has genuinely changed by four (four ball sheaves), and
`relHair_const`: every human relation in every configuration has one and the same hair (one hair
sheaf).

`nrrf800_answer` collects the clauses.
