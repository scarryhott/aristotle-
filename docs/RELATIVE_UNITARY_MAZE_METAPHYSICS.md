## Three relative closure levels

The reported analysis distinguishes three levels that must not be collapsed:

```text
periodic / phase return
relative-unitary return on a maintained fibre
lifted partition/maze record and its residual geometry
```

A phase can return and a relational unitary can close while a lifted record
retains a nonzero residue. The intended meaning is not that closure fails
absolutely. It is that return is relative to the level at which identity is
asked: a repeated phase, a returned fibre, and a partition geometry can carry
different but compatible closure verdicts.

This supports the open-foundation reading: a next local formation can be a
genuine continuation rather than a literal repetition even when a lower-level
return is exact.

## Translation, asymmetry, and inverse local/global readings

Let a local geometry `G` and a global axiom/topological presentation `A` be
related by candidate translations and returns:

```text
G --T_GA--> A
A --rho_AG--> G
```

The claim is not that `G` and `A` are fixed objects first and translated later.
Their apparent local/global distinction is itself relative to a prior
translation relation. A return can preserve a residue, orientation, or
asymmetry rather than erase it. Thus “inverse identity” means an auditable
returned relation, not literal identity of every local presentation.

The interaction is evaluated twice:

1. locally, by the chosen geometry/definition and its line, fibre, or record;
2. globally, by the compatibility of the translation/return cone and the
   resulting topological or axiomatic presentation.

Neither evaluation is metaphysically final. They are inverse perspectives on a
single relational interaction, and each can expose an obstruction hidden by the
other.

## Choice before predicted natural closure

An interactive choice supplies a translation, orientation, extension, or
return. It does not arrive pre-certified as natural. Its commutation and
recovery obligations make the cost of that choice visible. The predicted
topological closure is therefore conditional:

```text
chosen interaction
  -> audit of translation, return, orientation, and refinement
  -> natural relative closure | obstruction | OPEN
```

An “unnatural” perspective is not meaningless; it may return a dual local
aspect, exhibit a noncommuting refinement, or preserve a residue that blocks a
stronger identification. Such a result must remain in the evidence rather than
being normalized away.

## Maze lines, topology fold, and curvature

An open set of locally geometric maze lines can be read as a candidate topology
fold only through explicit returns. Translation may then present the fold as an
orientation, extension, or relative asymmetry. A unitary-curvature/maze-partition
claim requires separate realization theorems showing which line relation,
return relation, curvature quantity, and topology are being compared.

The existing finite maze is evidence for a bounded return-gated topology proxy.
It is not evidence that all curvature, partition, Turing, or topological
descriptions coincide.
