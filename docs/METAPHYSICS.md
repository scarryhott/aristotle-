## Foundational claim

The foundational claim is not merely that everything is related, nor merely that translations preserve already-defined mathematical objects. Its central question is:

> **Does there exist a translation independent of the selection of an origin language, whose relations themselves form reference frames, and can such translation relate formal identities across changing frames?**

The thesis proposes that **axioms are relative to their geometries/reference frames and translation is foundational to the natural existence available between such frames**. A reference frame is therefore not metaphysically prior to relation: local frames are formations within an origin-independent translational relation.

More strongly, translation is understood here in a relational sense prior to
the distinction between a fixed language, definition, institution, or local
topos. Those structures are local appearances of a translational relation, not
its absolute source. The present formal kernel does not yet begin at that prior
level; its required formal openings are recorded in
[Pre-institutional translation: formal openings](PREINSTITUTIONAL_TRANSLATION_OPENINGS.md).

```text
origin-independent translational relation
        ↓
relative relational formations / reference frames
        ↓
axiom_F ↔ geometry_F ↔ admitted equality_F
        ↓
relational definition
        ↓
translational completion / closure
```

The project does not require one absolute axiom system, geometry, language, or selected origin from which all others inherit identity. A local mathematical frame is a unified axiom-geometric formation. Another frame `G` may have a different form. Neither must first be reduced to a privileged third frame. Their common identity, when available, is disclosed relationally through admissible translation and return.

This is the claimed foundational novelty. Groupoid coherence, quotients, natural transformations, sections, universal properties, finite runtime certificates, and other classical constructions are downstream mathematical forms used to express and test consequences of this starting point.

## Truth-level equality, not equality of quality or quantity

Closure is about **truth at the relational level**. It does not assert that two
relative presentations have the same quality, quantity, syntax, coordinate,
measurement, or local definition.

Accordingly, the intended reading of local/global and `0`/`∞` is:

```text
local != global             as relative presentations
0 != ∞                      as relative presentations
local = global in closure   at truth-level relational identity
0 = ∞ in closure            at truth-level relational identity
```

The phrase **in closure** names the equality level at which the relation is
true. It does not collapse the poles into ordinary numerical identity. Their
relative difference remains available as orientation, residue, route, or the
basis of a further relation.

This is why translation need not first define both sides inside one selected
origin language. The truth relation is taken as prior to the local
presentations through which it appears. The formal programme tests conditional
witnesses of that claim; it does not yet prove language-prior truth as an
unconditional theorem.

See [Closure as a translational truth condition](TRUTH_CONDITION_TRANSLATIONAL_CLOSURE.md).

## Relation forms frames; frames do not freeze axiometry

“Relations form frames” does not mean that one completed relation permanently freezes an axiom system. A frame is a relative formation in an evolving translational structure. Its local axiometry can change as new relations, translations, returns, and completions alter the available perspective.

Accordingly, the experimental runtime may **freeze an evidence artifact** before a comparison so that the experiment cannot retrospectively rewrite it, but this causal freeze is not a metaphysical claim that axiometry itself is static.

The intended distinction is:

```text
experimental freeze = preserve what a particular episode actually produced
metaphysical closure = allow perspective/axiometry to evolve through auditable translation and return
```

Closure is therefore **interactive continual completion**: relational formations can become new frames and new bases for subsequent translation rather than terminating in one final canonical language.

## Natural existence

“Natural” is meant literally as well as metaphysically. In the formal translational frame, a chosen translation and return can satisfy the commuting relation

```text
W_m(T_lm u) = phi_lm(W_l u).
```

The interpretation is not that `F` and `G` are absolutely identified before this square is checked, nor that a finished truth-object is preserved unchanged. The square is a local formal witness of the truth condition for a further recoverable relation available **through** their translation. Translation therefore participates in constituting cross-frame relational identity.

Translation therefore participates in constituting cross-frame relational identity. A reference frame remains usable without becoming an origin. Any frame may locally present a returned identity, while coherent translation carries that truth-level relation into a further formation without requiring the local presentations to become literally identical.

### Choice, admission, cost, and non-circular naturality

The metaphysical reading must not turn naturality into a hidden global chooser.
Translation is the shared relation within which local formations can make
interactive choices. A choice is made first; its proposed return, orientation,
extension, or identification is then tested for commutation, reflection,
recovery, and independently generated consequences. Naturality is therefore a
constraint on a choice, not the mechanism that forces the choice to exist. The
current runtime records this as explicit obligations and counterexamples, not
as a scalar cost functional, entropy, quality, or quantity.

Naturality is therefore a truth condition on a choice, not the mechanism that
forces the choice to exist. **True admission must itself be related to its
natural choice.** A gate is not primarily a closed entropy barrier; it is the
interface that asks whether a chosen interaction can participate in an
auditable further relation.

```text
choice
  -> proposed translation
  -> independent return / consequence
  -> naturality / recovery / reflection audit
  -> admitted continuation | obstruction | OPEN
```

The Level-1 and Level-2 native controls enforce this anti-circularity by
separating a gate verdict from independent calibration and held-out external
consequences. A self-certified receipt or a leaked outcome cannot make an
admission true.

The requested **universal transformation cost** is correspondingly not a
sorting cost, entropy value, or cost of one syntax. Its target is the burden of
the relational transformation itself, independent of language or definition
basis. Formally, any eventual cost should factor through translational identity:

```text
presentation-level transformation
        -> closure/translational identity class
        -> transformation cost.
```

Thus admitted re-expressions of the same relational transformation should have
the same cost. The repository does not yet claim a universal scalar formula;
it currently records proof-relevant burdens—residue, obligations,
counterexamples, return requirements, abstentions, and external-audit costs—from
which such an invariant might later be derived.

## Relative axiom and geometry

An axiom is not metaphysically prior to geometry, nor geometry prior to axiom. Their role is relative and mutually disclosed inside a selected frame. A genuinely novel axiom-geometry must therefore first be admitted conditionally in its own unified manner before comparison with another frame.

To evaluate a novel frame `G` does not mean assuming that `G` is absolutely true. It means:

```text
if G is the current reference frame,
evaluate its equality and consequences under G;
then ask how G relates translationally to F;
allow completed relational return to participate in the next frame/basis.
```

This avoids treating an older frame `F` as a supposedly neutral metalanguage while also avoiding the opposite error of freezing `G` as a final origin.

## Equality, relational definition, and openness

The frame-conditional layer represents a reference frame by its admitted equality. A comparison becomes `GeomEquiv` when it preserves and reflects the equalities of the two frames:

```text
x ~_F y  ↔  T(x) ~_G T(y).
```

This formal equality transport is a conditional local witness. The metaphysical thesis is stronger: truth-level identity is not exhausted by preserving a fixed frame's equality forever, because a successful closure may translate into a further relation and a changed axiometric basis.

Resolution is relational factorization through that equality. A total question is open in a frame exactly when it separates occurrences that the frame itself equates. Openness is therefore not missing computation, non-selection, or a third truth value. It is conditional on the axiom geometry.

The separately reported NRRF633 result adds a downstream uniqueness theorem: within an existing translational frame, a relational definition that is returning and grounded is uniquely the equality of returned identity. Translation then forces that equality to be natural across languages. This theorem does not originate the foundational translation; it shows what relational definition follows once the natural translational structure is present.

## Translational completion and closure

Translation alone is not completion. A forward relational path may exist without the returns required to recover its relational identity. The operative runtime therefore distinguishes raw translation/reach from explicit return completion.

The bounded completion-maze experiment tests the conjunction

```text
completion ∧ LocalIVI_W ∧ exact reach/equality alignment
```

as its executable closure proxy. Before return, raw reach does not realize the frozen equality classes. After explicit return lines, the completed reach relation does. The resulting saturation topology and receipt are derived only after this completion.

The broader closure thesis is therefore interpreted as **interactive continual completion** rather than a one-time equality test. Closure does not preserve a finished truth unchanged: a truthful relation can translate into a further relation, whose completion becomes part of the next basis.

The bounded closure-native axiometric-evolution run makes this longitudinal form executable:

```text
F0 -> interaction -> completion -> derived F1
   -> changed-axiom interaction -> completion -> derived F2.
```

Successor frames are generated from completion lineage rather than pre-authored as the final truth. This is bounded architectural evidence, not a universal theorem.

## IVI and nontrivial relational identity

IVI—intangibly verified information—is the informational interpretation of naturally recoverable relational identity without requiring unique reconstruction of a local occurrence. Distinct presentations may remain distinct while participating in one returned truth-level relation.

Thus closure does not erase perspective. It asks whether relative difference can participate in a completed relational identity and then enter a further relation. IVI supplies the nontrivial informational side of that thesis; translational completion supplies the returned structural side.

## Value and credit as relative agreement

The motivating application is broader than formal mathematics. Reasoning minds may carry different local definitions, measurements, or perspectives of value and credit. The thesis does not require one mind's definition to become the privileged global definition.

Instead, agreement can be sought through relative translation:

```text
local value frame A
      ↕ translation / return
local value frame B
```

What counts as shared value is then the truth-level relational identity recoverable through admissible translation, not arbitrary equality with one origin's local scalar. Different quantities or qualities can remain locally distinct while their relation is true under translation. The bounded runtimes test the mathematical architecture of this distinction; they do not claim to have solved economic value or interpersonal agreement in general.

## 0 and infinity

The poles `0` and `∞` are relatively distinguishable orientations inside a closure relation. Their difference is presentation-relative; their equality is truth-level and closure-relative.

Thus the thesis is not ordinary arithmetic `0 = ∞`. It is:

```text
0 != ∞        as relative quantitative presentations
[0]_C = [∞]_C at the truth level of the closure relation.
```

Reversal can change orientation without destroying that truth-level relation when translation and return remain admissible. Distinction therefore survives inside relational identity; it can become residue or a further opening rather than being erased.

## Mathematical ASI

A mathematical superintelligence is a severe test of this foundation because it may generate axiom-geometries that were not selected by its verifier in advance and may continue changing them through reasoning.

The translational proposal does not ask such a system to remain permanently inside a human-selected geometry. A new frame is evaluated in its own unified axiom-geometric form; afterward, cross-frame translation is sought and tested for equality equivalence, downstream naturality, completion, relational return, and—where available—independent external consequences.

Verification therefore becomes the audit of truth-level relational identity across evolving mathematical frames rather than permanent allegiance to one static presentation or the preservation of one closed truth-object.

The research question is consequently not only whether an ASI can produce a correct theorem in Lean. It is whether mathematical truth remains auditable as the intelligence changes the perspectives and axiom-geometries through which it reasons and translates them into further relations.

## Formal boundary

The Lean modules establish conditional theorems about explicitly specified translational frames, equalities, returns, bridges, and runtime realizations. They do not prove an unconditional proposition that all reality must instantiate one fixed `TransFrame`, that `0 = ∞` in ordinary arithmetic, or that a language-independent universal transformation-cost scalar already exists.

That is intentional. The metaphysical thesis itself rejects the demand for one privileged absolute frame. The formal programme instead asks how much truth-level identity, naturality, relational definition, completion, and verification can be recovered **conditionally across relative axiom-geometries through translation into further relations**.

The thesis can therefore be summarized as:

```text
truth-level origin-independent translation
    → relational formations / relative frames
    → local axiom-geometries and presentations
    → natural choice / admission
    → translation + independent return
    → interactive continual completion
    → further relation / next perspective or axiometric basis
    → externally auditable consequence
    → next choice.
```

The Lean results and executable runtimes test and constrain consequences of this thesis; they do not silently convert the metaphysical starting point into an unconditional theorem.
