# Generative Axiom-Geometry Isolation Fixture

Status: **EXECUTED BOUNDED GENERATIVE PROXY**.

This bounded fixture runs three subprocess roles. Generator A searches generated permutation
groups. Generator B searches affine groups over finite prime fields. Each receives only the shared
abstract objective and its own precommitted context, constructs a local finite group, freezes an
admitted equality over two occurrence presentations, evaluates the total questions in that frame,
and solves the local involution problem.

Only after both artifacts are content-addressed does a third subprocess receive them. It enumerates
every basis bijection and both orientation maps, constructs `(T,phi,pi)`, and separately checks
equality equivalence and `W/E/J/C`/operation naturality. No translator is selected merely because
one exists.

The runtime also checks an order-eight `D4` versus `Q8` contrast. Their equality-fibre geometries
have bijective comparisons, but no comparison preserves multiplication. This is a naturality
obstruction, not `OpenIn`.

The committed raw artifacts are a separate assay, not inputs to the scripted positive lane. The
finite D4/S3 pair yields a cardinality `GeomEquiv` obstruction. The C6 word-language variant keeps
its explicitly non-finite occurrence domain and is reported outside the finite verifier rather
than normalized.

```bash
python3 experiments/generative_axiom_geometry_isolation.py --assert-reference
```

The output is a deterministic finite proxy. Subprocess and causal separation do not establish
epistemic independence, frontier-model capability, or actual ASI.

The initial freedom is narrow: each generator performs deterministic search only inside its own
precommitted permutation or affine family. The shared objective fixes order six, noncommutativity,
the order spectrum, generator relations, and the equality rule. No claim of unrestricted theorem
invention follows.

The exact causal boundary is:

```text
F_A, F_B, Q_A, Q_B frozen
  < content-addressed disclosure
  < candidate (T,phi,pi) enumeration
  < GeomEquiv preservation and reflection
  < W/E/J/C/operation naturality
  < question and held-out transfer.
```

Each frame is replayed under its own frozen admitted equality. A partial, rejected, obstructed, or
unselected comparison is not `OpenIn`; `OpenIn` appears only for a named frame/question with an
explicit frame-equal separating pair.

The protocol is falsified if a generator receives the other context/artifact through its declared
interface, either artifact changes after disclosure, `pi` or `T` can be detached from `phi`, an
exact comparison omits equality reflection, a claimed natural form fails any commuting operation,
or an unsupported/cardinality-blocked comparison is called open. The fresh raw-agent assay is a
separate boundary check and does not determine the scripted positive lane's PASS.
